# Front garden — landscaper overview

Based on SketchUp model `bastings-garden`, reviewed 22 November 2026. Dimensions in **millimetres**, levels as **RL in metres**, approximate unless stated. Left and right are as viewed from the street.

## Design at a glance

- **Scope:** the front area of the property, from the kerb up to the verandah. It covers the driveway and car bay, the bluestone entry stair and landings, terraced garden beds, a built-in seat, a focal bowl, the carport pergola, an arbor with a wire balustrade, the bin store and the side gate.
- **Character:** soft, layered spring/summer planting of green and silver foliage with restrained flower colour, held by low rendered walls and natural stone.
- **Retained:** the exposed stone walls on both sides of the driveway, the tall street hedge (bed about 5,700 × 1,500) and the climber on the side fence. Protect all three during works. Other existing retaining walls in the front garden are removed.

  - Four 140 posts.

## Possible materials

- **Paving and steps:** sawn bluestone with a honed or sawn finish and slip resistance suited to external steps. Steppers are natural-edge (split) bluestone.
- **Driveway:** light exposed-aggregate concrete with a pale cement and aggregate mix. Ask for a sample panel against the existing paving.
- **Pergola, arbor and seat:** plain, unpainted durable hardwood, for example spotted gum, ironbark or blackbutt, left to silver. Use stainless or galvanised fixings.
- **Battens:** hardwood or treated pine with a black stain, for example 42 × 42 to 66 × 45 with even gaps.
- **Balustrade:** galvanised or powder-coated steel posts and 316 stainless wire with tensioners.
- **Retaining walls:** rendered blockwork with drainage behind. Match the render colour to the house.
- **Beds:** fine organic mulch over improved garden soil.

## Planting

Plant counts are indicative modelled clumps, not a plant schedule. Sizes are as modelled at maturity.

- **English box (*Buxus sempervirens*):** low clipped hedging and balls, about 500–700 high and wide. About 24 clumps.
- **Crepe myrtle (*Lagerstroemia* sp.):** one multi-stem deciduous feature tree with blush-pink flowers and cinnamon bark, about 3,600 high × 3,800 spread.
- **Tractor-seat plant (*Ligularia reniformis*):** bold kidney-shaped leaves through the beds, about 500 high × 900 spread. About 25 clumps.
- **Bugle flower (*Ajuga reptans*):** a groundcover mat with blue spring spires, also planted between the steppers. About 200 high × 650 spread, about 98 clumps.
- **Silver Falls (*Dichondra argentea* 'Silver Falls'):** silver-grey groundcover trailing about 500 over the rendered walls. About 23 clumps.
- **Pergola climber:** deciduous, for partial cover. No species is specified. Options include ornamental grape (*Vitis vinifera* 'Purpurea') or wisteria, or Chinese star jasmine if evergreen cover is wanted. With wisteria, confirm the timber sizes can carry its weight.
- **Layering:** box frames the bed edges. Ligularia and ajuga fill the beds, and dichondra spills over the wall faces. The crepe myrtle and bowl act as focal points.
- **Check aspect:** ligularia prefers shade and steady moisture. Confirm the sun and shade pattern for each bed and substitute if needed.
- **Consider:** box blight risk. Japanese box (*Buxus microphylla* var. *japonica*) is a more tolerant alternative.

## Assumptions

- The model uses reasonable placeholder sizes where no dimensions were given: seat depth, post sections, rafter spacing and batten sizes.
- The balustrade wire spacing is not set. Confirm it against current balustrade requirements.
- Finished grades are provisional. The existing driveway is assumed to fall about 40 over 6,500 towards the street, and the crossfall is unknown.
- The new stair foot (−2,040 below the verandah) is in a different position from the existing stair foot (−1,800).
- The existing landing (about RL 40.190) is replaced by the new surfaces at RL 40.270 and RL 40.440. Its footprint alignment is approximate.
- Planting is shown established at spring/summer maturity, not at installation size.
- The car, the clearance envelope and the existing landing outline in the model are visual references only.

**Confirm before construction:** site set-out and levels, drainage behind the walls and across the driveway, footings for the pergola and arbor, wire balustrade compliance, the retained hedge and walls and how they meet the new work, and the plant selection for each bed's aspect.
