# IBC wicking bed — build overview

A self-watering strawberry bed made from a cut-down IBC tub, clad in Merbau. All dimensions in **millimetres**, from the SketchUp model labels; approximate unless stated.

## Build sequence

The Viewpoints step through the build: **1. Pine batten rings**, **2. Merbau decking**, **3. Capped bed**. Use **Layers** to hide the cladding and look inside.

## Base

- **Plinth:** treated pine sleepers, two long **1,240 × 200 × 50** and two end **940 × 200 × 50**, with recessed fixings.
- **Support pallet:** an inset pallet (runners, feet and a support deck) under the tub; its construction is assumed.

## Tub and wicking layers

- **Tub:** HDPE IBC tub with an open cut rim, inside its galvanised cage (rails at about **245**, **515** and **800** centres) and a black woven outer wrap.
- **Layers, bottom to top:** scoria reservoir, geotextile separator, growing soil, straw mulch.
- **Plumbing:** **50** fill riser with cap feeding a slotted distribution pipe; overflow outlet with its invert at **360**, extended through the front cladding.
- **Planting:** six strawberry crowns.

## Cladding

- **Batten rings:** lower, middle and upper rings of **70 × 20** pine (long battens **1,222**, end battens **982**), screwed through into the cage uprights.
- **Merbau cladding:** vertical boards **610** long, mostly **140 × 20**, with **117 × 20** boards at the ends of each run; countersunk screws into all three battens.
- **Capping:** mitred **90 × 20** Merbau cap boards (long caps **1,270**, end caps **1,070** to the long point) on **66 × 66 × 30** treated pine corner blocks.

## Confirm before building

- The tub's actual size and cut height, and the pallet under it.
- Overflow height against the reservoir depth.
- Board count and widths around the overflow outlet.
